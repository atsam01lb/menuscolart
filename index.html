Digital Menus, Explained

What Is A Digital Menu, And Why Restaurants Use One

A digital menu is a mobile-friendly, QR-code-accessible version of a restaurant's menu, designed, built and hosted by Colart Digital Marketing Agency. Guests scan a code or tap a link to browse dishes, prices and photos instantly, and select menus, like Glow Bites, even let customers build an order and check out straight to WhatsApp. Every menu on this page is a live example of what Colart builds for restaurants across Lebanon, the GCC and beyond.

Always Up To Date

Change a price, add a dish or mark something sold out in minutes. No reprinting, no waiting on a new batch of physical menus.

Instant QR Access

Guests scan a code on the table and the menu opens immediately in their browser. No app to download, no account to create.

Built Mobile First

Every menu is designed to load fast and look sharp on a phone, since that is where almost every guest is browsing from.

Order Straight To WhatsApp

Select menus let customers add items to a basket and send the order directly on WhatsApp, no third-party ordering app needed.

Built To Be Found

Each restaurant gets its own optimized page, so guests searching for the restaurant or its dishes online can find the menu directly.

Lower Cost, Less Waste

Cut recurring printing costs and paper waste while keeping a more professional, consistent presentation across every table.

Common Questions

Digital Menu Questions, Answered

Everything restaurant owners and guests usually ask about the digital menus Colart Digital Marketing Agency builds.

What is a digital menu?

A digital menu is an online version of a restaurant's menu that guests open by scanning a QR code or tapping a link, instead of reading a printed card. It shows the same dishes, prices and categories, and can be updated instantly whenever something changes.

How do digital menus help restaurants get found online?

Each restaurant on menus.colartdigitalmarketingagency.com gets its own dedicated, search-optimized page, so when someone searches for the restaurant name or its dishes, the menu itself can appear in the results, not just a social media profile.

Can customers order directly from a digital menu?

On some of the menus built by Colart, yes. Guests can add items to a basket, choose pickup or delivery, and send the full order straight to the restaurant on WhatsApp, without installing a separate ordering app.

Do digital menus work without an app?

Yes. Every digital menu Colart builds runs directly in the guest's mobile browser after a QR scan or link tap. There is nothing to download and nothing to install.

How much does a digital menu from Colart cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the menu and the features needed, such as WhatsApp ordering or multiple languages. Restaurants can reach out to Colart Digital Marketing Agency directly to get a quote for their own digital menu.

How do I get a digital menu for my restaurant?

Contact Colart Digital Marketing Agency by WhatsApp or email to share your current menu and branding. Colart designs, builds and hosts the digital menu, then hands you a link and QR code ready to place on your tables.
